Our Story

Founded in 2020, Fresh Bite began with a simple idea to bring healthy, flavorful meals to everyone. We use locally sourced ingredients and combine them with Asian cooking traditions to create dishes full of color and life.

Our Vision

To become Uganda’s most loved healthy Ugandan restaurant.

Our Mission

To make healthy eating enjoyable, accessible, and inspiring.

Meet our Chef

Chef Amir, believes food should be both beautiful and nourishing. His expertise ensures every meal is balanced with taste, color, and care.
